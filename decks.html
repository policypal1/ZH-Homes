Wood or Composite?

Compare the tradeoffs before you decide

You do not need to know the answer before contacting us. We can talk through the choice based on your budget, maintenance expectations, appearance, and how long you plan to stay in the home.

Natural & Familiar

Wood Decking

  • Natural appearance and familiar feel
  • Often a lower initial investment
  • Multiple stain and finish options
  • Requires ongoing cleaning, staining, or sealing
Lower Maintenance

Composite Decking

  • No regular staining or sealing
  • Consistent color and finish options
  • Resists splintering and common moisture issues
  • Higher initial investment than many wood options

Deck Project FAQ

Common questions before requesting an estimate

How much does a new deck cost?

Deck pricing depends on size, height, access, framing, stairs, railings, material choice, site conditions, and design details. The most useful next step is a project-specific estimate rather than a generic national square-foot number.

Do you build both wood and composite decks?

Yes. We can discuss wood and composite options and help you compare initial cost, appearance, maintenance, and long-term priorities.

Can you help us choose the size and layout?

Yes. Think about the furniture, grill, number of people, stairs, and how you want to move between the house and yard. Those details help shape a practical layout.

Does my deck require a permit?

Permit requirements depend on the deck height, location, and project details. We can discuss what applies to the proposed project and clarify permit responsibility in the project scope.

Can a deck be built on a sloped yard?

Many decks are designed for sloped properties. The slope, access, elevation, footing conditions, stairs, and drainage need to be reviewed before a reliable plan and estimate can be prepared.

What areas do you serve?

ZH Homes serves Salem, Keizer, and nearby Oregon communities. Larger projects may be considered farther into the Willamette Valley depending on scope and schedule.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Submit the project basics and any helpful photos. Zach will review the request and contact you with the clearest next step.
