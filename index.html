FAQ

Common questions before getting started.

Here are a few quick answers customers usually want before requesting an estimate.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. ZH Homes can review the project, ask for photos if needed, and help you understand the best next step.

What areas do you serve?

ZH Homes is based around Salem and can serve nearby areas including Keizer, Dallas, Portland, Eugene, Grand Ronde, Mill City, and surrounding communities depending on job size.

What kind of work do you take on?

Common work includes remodeling, construction projects, handyman repairs, rental property work, punch lists, interior updates, and custom jobs.

Are you licensed?

Yes. ZH Homes is listed with Oregon CCB #260679. You can keep this license number visible on the site for trust.

Can I send photos of the project?

Yes. The quote form includes an image upload field so customers can send a project photo along with their message. Keep uploads under 10MB.

What is the best way to reach you?

Call, text, email, or use the quote form. For quick job questions, many customers start by texting photos and a short description.
