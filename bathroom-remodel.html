Bathroom Remodel FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask Before Remodeling a Bathroom

Direct answers about pricing, timelines, hidden problems, waterproofing, and the 24-hour estimate offer.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

Pricing depends on the bathroom size, current condition, layout changes, plumbing or electrical work, shower and waterproofing scope, materials, and finish choices. A walkthrough lets ZH Homes price the actual room instead of giving you a generic number that may not match the project.

How do I avoid the final bill growing after the work starts?

Start with a clear written scope, defined allowances, and a process for approving changes before extra work is performed. Hidden conditions can still exist, but the goal is to make the known scope and pricing clear before demolition.

How long will my bathroom be out of use?

The timeline depends on project size, material availability, inspections, and what is found after demolition. Before work starts, the schedule and bathroom-access plan should be discussed so your household knows what to expect.

What happens if you find rot, mold, plumbing, or electrical problems?

Hidden conditions are documented and discussed before the scope changes. You should understand what was found, why it matters, and what it will cost before additional work moves forward.

Can you convert my tub into a walk-in shower?

Yes. Tub-to-shower conversions can make the bathroom easier to enter, easier to clean, and better suited to daily use. The walkthrough helps assess plumbing, waterproofing, dimensions, and finish options.

How quickly can I get an estimate?

Request the free walkthrough through the three-step form. ZH Homes will follow up about the visit and the written 24-hour estimate offer, subject to current weekly scheduling availability.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

ZH Homes lists Oregon CCB #260679 and operates as a licensed, bonded, and insured Oregon contractor.

What areas do you serve?

ZH Homes serves Salem, Keizer, Silverton, and nearby Oregon communities. Availability outside those areas depends on project scope, location, and the current schedule.
