Frequently Asked Questions

Before you request an estimate.

What kinds of deck projects do you take on?

New deck construction, deck replacement and rebuilds, repairs, railings, stairs, and related exterior carpentry can be reviewed through the project form.

Do I need to know the exact size or material?

No. Approximate measurements and material preferences are useful, but you can select “Not sure yet” and explain what you want the space to accomplish.

Why does the form ask for a budget range?

Deck projects vary substantially by size, site conditions, material, structure, and finish. A range helps determine project fit and makes the first conversation more productive.

Can I send photos by text?

Yes. You can upload up to four photos in the form or text additional photos to (503) 910-5466.

How quickly will I hear back?

Zach checks his phone regularly during the workday and typically responds within a few hours Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Response time may vary while he is on a job site.
