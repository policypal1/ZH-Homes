Free Deck Estimate FAQ

What to Expect Before Building a New Deck

Direct answers about the free visit, written estimate, materials, permits, and service area.

Is the on-site deck estimate really free?

Yes. The property visit and project estimate are free, and there is no obligation to hire ZH Homes.

When will I receive the written estimate?

ZH Homes will provide the written estimate within 48 hours after the in-person property visit.

What happens during the site visit?

We review the yard, access, existing conditions, approximate measurements, layout, material preferences, and how you want to use the finished deck.

How much does a new deck cost?

Pricing depends on size, height, site access, framing, material choice, railings, stairs, site conditions, and design. The free site visit allows us to prepare a project-specific written estimate.

Should I choose wood or composite decking?

Wood often has a lower initial price and a natural appearance, but it needs ongoing maintenance. Composite usually costs more upfront but reduces regular staining and sealing. We can compare both during the visit.

Do I need a permit?

Requirements vary by location, deck height, size, and design. Permit and inspection responsibilities will be clarified in the written project scope before construction begins.

Can a deck be built on a sloped yard?

Many sloped properties can support a well-designed deck. Elevation, footings, drainage, access, stairs, and structural requirements need to be reviewed on site.

What areas do you serve?

ZH Homes serves Salem, Keizer, Silverton, and nearby Oregon communities. Availability outside those areas depends on the project location and scope.
