Bathroom Remodel FAQ

Questions homeowners usually have before they call.

A few quick answers about cost, timing, scope, and getting started.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

It depends heavily on the size of the room, how much is being replaced, whether plumbing or layout changes are needed, material choices, and what is found behind the existing finishes. The estimate process is meant to turn that uncertainty into a clear project scope and price.

How do we keep the project from constantly going over budget?

The best protection is a clear written scope before work starts. Materials, allowances, and what is or is not included should be discussed up front, and any new work should be talked through before it is added.

How long will my bathroom be out of use?

Timing depends on the size and complexity of the remodel. A focused update can move much faster than a full gut remodel. ZH Homes can talk through the expected sequence and what the project means for bathroom access before scheduling the work.

What if you find hidden rot, mold, plumbing, or electrical problems?

Hidden conditions are one of the biggest unknowns in remodeling. If something unexpected is uncovered, the next step is to document it, explain what it changes, and discuss the options before additional work moves forward.

Do you offer free bathroom estimates?

Yes. Start with the quote form, a call, or a text. Photos are optional but helpful. ZH Homes can then confirm the next step for reviewing the bathroom and preparing an estimate.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes. ZH Homes is licensed, bonded, and insured in Oregon. CCB #260679.
