Deck FAQ

Common questions before getting started.

A few direct answers before you request an estimate.

How much does a new deck cost?

Pricing depends on size, height, access, framing, stairs, railings, material choice, site conditions, and design details. A project-specific estimate is more useful than a generic national square-foot number.

Do you build both wood and composite decks?

Yes. We can discuss both options and help you compare initial price, appearance, maintenance, and long-term priorities.

Can you help choose the size and layout?

Yes. Furniture, grills, number of people, stairs, yard access, and how you want to move between the house and yard all help shape the layout.

Does my deck require a permit?

Permit requirements depend on deck height, location, and project details. We can discuss what applies and clarify permit responsibility in the project scope.

Can a deck be built on a sloped yard?

Many decks can be designed for sloped properties. Elevation, access, footing conditions, stairs, and drainage need to be reviewed before a reliable plan and estimate are prepared.

What areas do you serve?

ZH Homes serves Salem, Keizer, and nearby Oregon communities. Larger projects may be considered farther into the Willamette Valley depending on scope and schedule.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Complete the estimate form and include any helpful photos. We will review the request before contacting you with the clearest next step.
