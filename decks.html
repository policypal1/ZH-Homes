Deck FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask Before Building a Deck

Direct answers about pricing, materials, design, timing, permits, and service areas.

How much does a new deck cost?

Pricing depends on size, height, site access, framing, material choice, railings, stairs, site conditions, and custom features. A project-specific estimate is more useful than a generic national square-foot number.

Should I choose wood or composite decking?

Wood usually offers a natural appearance and a lower initial price on many projects, but it requires ongoing maintenance. Composite generally costs more upfront but reduces regular staining and sealing. We can help you compare appearance, maintenance, and long-term priorities.

How long does it take to build a deck?

Timing depends on the size and complexity of the design, material availability, weather, permit requirements, inspections, site access, and the current schedule. The expected timeline is discussed before the project is scheduled.

Do I need a permit?

Requirements vary by location, deck height, size, and design. Any permit and inspection responsibilities are clarified in the written project scope before construction begins.

Can you help me design the deck?

Yes. The consultation considers furniture, grills, number of people, stairs, railings, yard access, elevation, materials, and how you want to move between the house and yard.

Can a deck be built on a sloped yard?

Many sloped properties can support a well-designed deck. Elevation, footing conditions, drainage, access, stairs, and structural requirements need to be reviewed before a reliable plan is prepared.

What areas do you serve?

ZH Homes serves Salem, Keizer, Silverton, and nearby Oregon communities. Project availability outside those cities depends on scope, location, and the current schedule.

Do you provide deck estimates?

Yes. Complete the step-by-step form or call us. The project details help us confirm fit and prepare for a more useful follow-up conversation.
