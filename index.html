FAQ

Common questions before getting started.

A few quick answers before you reach out.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Send the basics and, when possible, a few photos. That usually gives enough context to talk through the next step.

What areas do you serve?

Most work is around Salem and nearby communities. Larger projects can reach farther into the Willamette Valley depending on scope and schedule.

What kind of work do you take on?

The best fit is usually small remodels, repairs, punch-list work, rental turnover items, interior updates, exterior upgrades, and custom jobs that do not fit neatly into one category.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Licensed, bonded, and insured. Oregon CCB #260679.

Can I send photos of the project?

Yes. The quote form includes an image upload field. Photos are optional, but they usually make it easier to understand the job.

What is the best way to reach you?

Call, text, email, or use the quote form. For quick job questions, many customers start by texting photos and a short description.
