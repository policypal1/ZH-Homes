Bathroom Remodel FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask Before Hiring a Bathroom Contractor

Direct answers about pricing, timelines, hidden problems, decisions, pressure, and what happens after you request an estimate.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

Pricing depends on the bathroom size, current condition, layout changes, plumbing or electrical work, shower and waterproofing scope, materials, and finish choices. A walkthrough lets ZH Homes price the actual room instead of giving you a generic number that may not match the project.

Will the price change after demolition starts?

Hidden conditions can exist in any remodel, especially behind walls or under wet areas. The safer approach is a clear written scope, defined allowances, and approval before additional work moves forward so a surprise condition does not automatically become a surprise charge.

How long will my bathroom be out of use?

The timeline depends on project size, material availability, inspections, and what is found after demolition. Before work starts, the schedule and bathroom-access plan should be discussed so your household knows what to expect.

What happens if you find rot, mold, plumbing, or electrical problems?

Hidden conditions are documented and discussed before the scope changes. You should understand what was found, why it matters, and what it will cost before additional work moves forward.

Do I need to know exactly what tile, fixtures, or layout I want before I call?

No. Many homeowners know what they dislike long before they know the exact solution. Start with what is not working. The walkthrough can help narrow the practical options, tradeoffs, and next decisions without requiring you to design the whole bathroom first.

Do I have to sign anything during the walkthrough?

No high-pressure decision should be required just to understand your project. The walkthrough is the starting point for clarifying the bathroom, scope, and estimate so you can review the information and decide what makes sense next.

What areas do you serve?
