How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

The range changes with room size, existing damage, plumbing or layout changes, and finish choices. ZH Homes starts with your goals and the existing room, then builds an estimate around the actual scope instead of giving a one-size-fits-all number.

How long will my bathroom be out of use?

A focused update or same-footprint conversion is usually simpler than a full gut remodel. Older homes can reveal water damage, dry rot, or uneven framing. The expected schedule and likely downtime are discussed before work is scheduled.

What if this is our only bathroom?

Mention that in your request. It affects sequencing and the project plan, so it is important to talk through usable periods and unavoidable downtime before work begins.

Do bathroom remodels need permits in Salem?

Plumbing, electrical, mechanical, and structural changes may require permits. The exact requirements depend on the work. You can also review the City of Salem’s official permit guidance before starting.
