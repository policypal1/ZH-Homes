Accessibility Statement

This page explains ZH Homes’ accessibility goal and gives visitors a direct way to get help if any part of the website is difficult to use.

Effective Date: June 12, 2026

Business: ZH Homes

Website: zh-homes.vercel.app

1. Our Accessibility Goal

ZH Homes wants this website to be accessible and usable for visitors, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom tools, voice control, or other assistive technology.

We aim to follow practical accessibility principles, including readable text, keyboard access, visible focus states, descriptive page structure, form labels, text alternatives for images, and clear contact options.

2. Help If the Website Is Not Working for You

If you have trouble using the website, reading content, uploading project images, submitting the quote form, or accessing any page, please contact us directly. We can provide the same information or help with your quote request another way.

Phone/Text: (503) 910-5466

Location: Salem, Oregon and surrounding areas

Website: Use the quote form or contact options on the main website.

3. Accessibility Improvements Made

As part of this website update, we added or improved the following accessibility-related items:

  • A skip link for keyboard users
  • Visible keyboard focus styling
  • Reduced-motion support for visitors who prefer less animation
  • Footer links to Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Accessibility pages
  • Clear labels for quote form fields
  • Additional description text for the image upload field
  • A direct phone/text option for users who cannot use the form

4. Ongoing Work

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Automated scans can help identify issues, but they do not catch everything. Manual testing with keyboard navigation, screen readers, and real user feedback is also important.

If you notice an accessibility problem, please let us know what page you were on, what issue happened, and what device or assistive technology you were using if you are comfortable sharing that information.

5. Third-Party Content

Some parts of the website may use third-party services, such as maps, hosting, forms, or other embedded tools. ZH Homes does not fully control the accessibility of third-party services, but we try to provide alternate ways to contact us when needed.
